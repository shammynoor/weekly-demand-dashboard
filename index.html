Consult mode categories

Create your own consultation modes, such as Telephone, Face to face or Video. Each slot type can have one consult mode independently of urgency and clinician type.

Consult modes

Click Save after editing. Other computers load the saved categories when they refresh the website. Unsaved edits stay only in this browser. Unassigned slot types appear in an “Uncategorised” category.

Counts are based on rows in the supplied exports. Future dates may contain unbooked slots. CSV reports are read from your OneDrive into browser memory; they are not uploaded to GitHub. Category assignments sync to your private OneDrive application folder. The source CSV folder is remembered in your private OneDrive application settings and loaded on every computer.
