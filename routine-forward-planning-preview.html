Mock forecast only; no patient or OneDrive data is loaded. Appointments means the unbooked inventory across the future diary at the start of the day (including held-back slots). New bookings consume that inventory. The gap shown is remaining inventory after predicted bookings, not same-day appointment capacity. Waiting times are illustrative assumptions, not derived from actual appointment dates. Once inventory reaches zero, wait is shown as “No capacity.” The days-until-full figure counts forecast working days; weekends are not displayed. The All view pools appointment volumes, while its illustrative wait is not a production pooled-diary forecast.
