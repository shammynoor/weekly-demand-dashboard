# UI conventions

## Mini toggles

The user calls compact switch controls **mini toggles**. Use a 30 × 18 px switch with a 14 px thumb and 12 px labels. Keep related mini toggles side by side horizontally beneath their associated slider, with a small gap between controls. Preserve keyboard focus and accessible switch labels. Reuse `.mini-toggle-row` in `index.html` for future mini toggle requests.
